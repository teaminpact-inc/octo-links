Who can use Octo

You must be at least 13 years old, or older if your country requires it. Keep your sign-in secure and use one account for yourself.

Community rules

We have no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users. In particular, you must not:

  • Use a display name that is hateful, sexual, violent, threatening or harassing, or that pretends to be someone else or shares someone's personal information
  • Harass, bully, threaten or stalk other people, including through link requests, reactions or forecast alerts
  • Send spam or link requests to people who don't know you
  • Break the law or help someone else do so

Reporting and blocking

  • Report: open the person's octopus, or tap ··· on a link request, and choose Report.
  • Block: from the same places. You disappear from each other's tank, and the blocked person can't send you a request or use your invite links. They aren't notified. You can unblock people in Home › ··· › Blocked people.
  • You can also email support@teaminpact.com.

We review every report within 24 hours. When something breaks these rules, we remove it, which can mean resetting a display name or deleting the account, and we may do so without warning.

Purchases

Items and characters are one-time purchases through the App Store or Google Play. Payments and refunds follow the store's rules. Use Restore purchases to get your items back on a new device or after signing up again with the same Apple ID or Google account.

Moods and forecasts

Octo is for sharing how you feel with people you choose. It is not medical or mental health advice. If you or someone else is in danger, contact your local emergency services.

Your account and data

You can delete your account at any time in the app (how). How we handle your data is in our Privacy Policy.

Changes and contact

We may update these terms and will show the new effective date here. Octo is provided as is, to the extent the law allows. Questions: support@teaminpact.com.
